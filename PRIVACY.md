# Cookie & Privacy Notice
## Data Collection & Storage
This application **does not use cookies** and **does not collect or transmit personal data** to any server.

### What We Store Locally
The app uses browser **localStorage** (not cookies) to save your preferences on your device only:

- **Zoom level** - Your preferred display size
- **Selected leagues** - Which sports leagues you want to see
- **Refresh interval** - How often you want scores to update
- **Team visibility** - Your favorite teams and hidden teams

This data:
- ✅ Stays on your device only
- ✅ Is never transmitted to any server
- ✅ Is not used for tracking or profiling
- ✅ Can be cleared anytime via the Settings menu

### Third-Party Data
**ESPN API**: This app fetches live sports data from ESPN's public API. All sports data (scores, statistics, team information) is:
- Owned by ESPN and/or its licensors
- Retrieved in real-time for display purposes only
- Not stored or collected by this application
- Subject to ESPN's Terms of Use

**No Tracking**: We do not use:
- ❌ Google Analytics or similar analytics tools
- ❌ Advertising cookies or pixels
- ❌ User profiling or behavioral tracking
- ❌ Social media tracking scripts
- ❌ Any third-party data collection services

### Legal Compliance
Under GDPR, ePrivacy Directive, and similar privacy laws, **strictly necessary storage** (like saving user preferences for functionality) does not require consent. This application only uses such necessary local storage.

### Your Rights
You maintain full control over your data:
- **Access**: All data is stored in your browser's localStorage
- **Deletion**: Clear all settings via the Settings menu or browser tools
- **Portability**: Data is stored in standard JSON format in localStorage

### Questions?
This is an educational project with no commercial purpose. We respect your privacy by design - we simply don't collect data.

For more information, see the License tab.
